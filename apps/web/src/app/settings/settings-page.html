Timer

Inspection counts 15 s down on the timer once the cube matches the scramble (no penalty is applied). A new session records the values it starts with.

On a phone, the camera's picture stays at the top of the Timer page with the scramble over it, and the scramble alone while the camera is off, so that both stay in view as the page scrolls. Off, the scramble, the time and the picture come one under the other, as before.

Camera

What the camera is asked for; a camera that is on opens again with it. Camera settings, on the Timer page, show what the camera claims next to the frame rate it measures: some claim 60 fps and deliver 30. “Exactly 60 fps” fails on a camera without a 60 fps mode, which then opens at its best rate and says so.

The recording's bitrate at the resolution and frame rate above, and about what an attempt's two clips take at it (“Best” counts 30 fps, which every camera measured so far delivers; 60 fps takes 1.5 times as much). Standard is plenty for the hands and the cube; High and Maximum fill the browser's storage (Storage, above) two and three times as fast. A recording under way starts again with the change.
