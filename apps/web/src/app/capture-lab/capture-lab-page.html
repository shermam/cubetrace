Capture lab

The capture pipeline on its own: the chosen camera is encoded in a worker into the last 90 s kept in memory, and any of it can be cut without re-encoding. The counters come once per second. Mux and save writes the last seconds as an MP4 and its frame times into a scratch folder of the browser's storage, replaced at every save, and plays the MP4 from there. Keep this tab in front while it records.

Sync check

It says how far the camera lags the cube, or why it cannot tell, and what measuring each frame cost the capture worker.
